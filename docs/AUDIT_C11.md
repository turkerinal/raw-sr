# Audit C11: Published Images & Root-Cause Diagnosis of Resolution Loss

This audit investigates why the published `raw_sr` comparison images show almost no visible before/after resolution gain, produces an honest pixel-aligned 4-panel visual comparison set, and diagnoses quantitatively where resolution is lost in the current classical pipeline.

### 1.2 Plain-Language Summary of What Is Wrong
1. **The README hero (`compare_4x_crop.png`) is a developer regression check, not a user-facing before/after:** Middle and Right are two SR builds that differ by 5.3 grey levels (out of 255), so they look identical. Meanwhile, the Left panel (`frames[0]`) is **not** raw CFA and is shifted by **39 HR pixels** relative to the SR panels because `compare_versions.py` hardcodes `frames[0]` instead of `frames[res.reference_index]`.
2. **The second README image (`c4_4x_tex1_sbs.png`) also compares two SR versions** (before vs after the VST MAP-IBP prox), not single-frame vs SR.
3. **The HDR+ showcase (`hdrplus_6G7M_4x_sbs.png`) is 70% blown out to pure white** because `sr_acceptance.py` hardcodes `exposure=4.0` regardless of burst brightness.

### 2.2 Five Diagnosed Bottlenecks

1. **Demosaic-before-fusion destroys aliased HF and injects false colour:**
   Per-frame DCB demosaicing interpolates away sub-Nyquist Bayer aliasing before frames are fused. On the synthetic target, direct Bayer `cfa_drizzle` beats `current_sr` by **+4.23 dB at 2×** and **+2.34 dB at 4×** (vs sensor GT; +2.28 dB vs lens GT at 4×) and completely removes the false-colour moiré on the Siemens star (`docs/audit-c11/synthetic_star_4x.png`), resolving spokes noticeably closer to the centre.
2. **Sparse drizzle footprint (`pixfrac=0.5`) vs IBP/MAP prox smoothing:**
   At 4× with 9 frames, shrinking `pixfrac` from `1.0 → 0.5 → 0.3` leaves unfilled subpixel gaps (creating the visible 4×4 dot/grid pattern on `HDR+ 0919`, `0382`, `6G7M`) and *lowers* standalone drizzle MTF50 (`0.336 → 0.301 → 0.213`) while raising flat noise (`0.00026 → 0.00230`). The 1-sweep VST MAP-IBP prox cleans up that noise (`0.00102 → 0.00025`) and recovers MTF50 to `0.381`, but only provides a modest `+13%` MTF50 gain over single bicubic (`0.338 → 0.381`) and **cannot invert the sensor-aperture + lens PSF**.
3. **Registration accuracy vs required sub-pixel precision:**
   For `ratio = 2 / 3 / 4`, Nyquist sub-pixel placement requires registration accuracy `< 0.25 / 0.167 / 0.125` LR px. On `own 9-frame burst` (`0.034 px`), `HDR+ 0919` (`0.051 px`), and `HDR+ 0382` (`0.049 px`), ECC registration meets the 4× requirement. However, on `HDR+ 6G7M` (`0.132 px` mean / `0.200 px` max) and especially `HDR+ 33TJ` (`0.304 px` mean / `0.573 px` max), registration error **exceeds the 4× precision limit**, explaining `33TJ`'s negative held-out gain.
4. **Sub-pixel diversity of real bursts (`shift mod 1`):**
   An 8–9 frame handheld burst physically cannot fill all 16 sub-pixel bins of a 4× grid:

   | Burst | Frames | 2×2 Bins Occupied | 4×4 Bins Occupied | Mean Torus NND | Align Residual Mean / Max (LR px) |
   |---|---:|---|---|---:|---|
   | Own 9-frame burst | 9 | 4/4 | 6/16 | 0.143 | 0.0337 / 0.0646 |
   | HDR+ `0919` | 8 | 3/4 | 5/16 | 0.114 | 0.0513 / 0.0936 |
   | HDR+ `0382` | 8 | 3/4 | 6/16 | 0.200 | 0.0491 / 0.0631 |
   | HDR+ `33TJ` (short) | 9 | 4/4 | 6/16 | 0.173 | **0.3036 / 0.5729** |
   | HDR+ `6G7M` (short) | 9 | 4/4 | 7/16 | 0.189 | **0.1324 / 0.1998** |

   Because only 5–7 of 16 bins are occupied at 4×, isotropic `pixfrac=0.5` scatter leaves periodic density holes (visible 4×4 grid).
5. **Noise reduction & real-edge MTF50 (the untapped astrophotography advantage):**
   - **Real-edge MTF50 (4×):** On `own 9-frame burst`, `single_bicubic = 0.2204` vs `current_sr = 0.2185 cyc/LR px` (`−0.9%`); on `HDR+ 0919`, `single_bicubic = 0.2408` vs `current_sr = 0.2313 cyc/LR px` (`−4.0%`). **Current `raw_sr` delivers zero net MTF50 sharpness gain on real high-contrast edges!**
   - **Noise reduction (stacking SNR gain):** Stacking lowers linear noise dramatically:
     - `own 9-frame burst`: single `σ = 0.000941` (`SNR 40.1 dB`) → stack `σ = 0.000203` (`SNR 53.4 dB`), a **4.64× (+13.3 dB)** noise reduction.
     - `HDR+ 0919`: single `σ = 0.005058` (`SNR 36.7 dB`) → stack `σ = 0.001533` (`SNR 47.1 dB`), a **3.30× (+10.4 dB)** noise reduction.
   - **Why this matters:** In astrophotography, stacking alone produces a *cleaner* image, not a dramatically sharper one; the visible resolution jump comes from **regularized deconvolution (RL-TV) + multiscale sharpening** that the 3.3×–4.6× quieter stacked master can sustain without noise blow-up (proven above: `current_sr + RL(8)` hits `0.534 cyc/LR px` and *gains* GT PSNR, while `single_bicubic + RL(8)` *loses* `−0.85 dB` GT PSNR).
